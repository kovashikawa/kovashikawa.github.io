Great news! After receiving an extended YouTube API quota, my [Spotify2YouTube](https://github.com/kovashikawa/spotify2youtube) project is officially back on track!

Here’s part of the approval message I received:

> "Your application has been approved, based on the information you provided, and your representation that your use of YouTube API Service is in full compliance with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.  
>
> We have increased your quota by 990,000 units for a period of 6 months. The total quota is now 1,000,000 units per day."

Huge thanks to the YouTube API Quota team for the trust. 

For developers considering a similar project: the review process took ~8 months and several email exchanges, so plan accordingly.
